PDFGenius is a straightforward PDF toolkit that works entirely on your device — no account, no cloud upload, no internet connection required to use any tool.

TOOLS INCLUDED
• Merge — combine multiple PDFs into a single document, in the order you choose.
• Split — pull out just the pages you need into a new PDF.
• Compress — shrink a PDF's file size with an adjustable quality level, so you can see exactly how much space you save.
• Convert to Images — turn every page of a PDF into a standalone PNG image.
• OCR Text Extraction — pull readable text out of scanned or image-based PDF pages using on-device text recognition. No page ever leaves your phone to do this.

HOW YOUR FILES ARE HANDLED
Every tool in PDFGenius processes your documents locally on your device. Imported and created PDFs are kept in a local library on your phone so you can find them again later.

WHAT'S NOT IN YET
Password protection is on our roadmap but not available in this release — the tool is visible in the app with a clear "Coming Soon" notice rather than a feature that silently does nothing.

PDFGenius is built for people who need quick, reliable PDF utilities without signing up for a service or trusting a stranger's server with their documents.
